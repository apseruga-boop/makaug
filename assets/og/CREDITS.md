# Share-card photo credits

The default collage and the category share cards crop and overlay these photographs with the makaug logo and brand text. They are adaptations of Wikimedia Commons files. Each source is Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike. The adapted cards are offered under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with attribution to the photographers below.

These are not listing photos and they are not the expiring TikTok covers the site refuses as listing media. A property share card uses that listing's own hosted photo when one is usable, and falls back to a category card when it is not.
